The Revenant

The Revenant is a Western survival drama released in 2015 and directed by Alejandro González Iñárritu. The film grossed approximately $533 million at the global box office. It is a semi-biographical epic based on Michael Punke’s 2002 novel, and explores true-life experiences of frontiersman Hugh Glass in 1823. Iñárritu’s direction, alongside the film’s historical context, sets the stage for a cinematic experience that is a quintessential example of the endurance of the human spirit and the brutal realities of the American frontier.

The story is set in the snowy wilderness of the Louisiana Purchase, where Hugh Glass (played by Leonardo DiCaprio) serves as a scout for a fur-trapping expedition. After being brutally mauled by a grizzly bear, Glass is left in the care of his young son and two hunters, including the mercenary John Fitzgerald (played by Tom Hardy). Fearing an impending attack by Arikara warriors, Fitzgerald murders Glass’s son and buries Glass alive. The plot then follows Glass’s miraculous survival as he crawls through hundreds of miles of frozen terrain, driven by rage and a desire for revenge.

The creative elements of the film distinguish it as a masterpiece of "Mise-en-scène." DiCaprio delivers a visceral, physically demanding performance that relies more on the portrayal of emotion rather than dialogue. Cinematographer Emmanuel Lubezki famously used only natural light and period-correct firelight, utilizing extremely wide lenses to capture the feeling of isolation from the landscape. The mood is cold and somber, supported by a minimalist score and lots of sound effects such as rushing water and wind. Dream sequences involving Glass’s late wife and surreal imagery, like a bird coming out of a chest, add a layer of Native American spirituality to the film's wild feel.

In conclusion, Alejandro González Iñárritu was very successful in his purpose of creating an immersive, sensory-driven epic. By insisting on filming in chronological order and using natural lighting, the barrier between the audience and the screen was removed, making the environment feel more like a character than a setting. Evidence of this success is found in the opening battle sequence, making the violence feel personal and immediate by using the film's techniques. Between the straightforward plot and excellent acting and techniques, The Revenant is an iconic example of a modern western film.
